Audiogram, pure-tone averages and WHO hearing grade

Enter air- and bone-conduction thresholds in dB HL. The audiogram uses the ASHA (1990) symbol set and proportions. Averages, air–bone gaps and the WHO (2021) grade update as you type.

Thresholds (dB HL)

Type a number from −10 to 120, or NR for no response at the audiometer limit. Leave blank if not tested. Bone conduction is tested from 250 to 4000 Hz.
